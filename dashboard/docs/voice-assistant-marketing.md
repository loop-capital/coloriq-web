# ColorIq Voice Assistant
### AI-Powered Bowl-Side Consultation

---

## What It Is

A push-to-talk voice assistant built into ColorIq that answers formulation questions in real-time — right at the bowl, while your hands are in gloves.

**Tap the mic → Ask your question → Get an answer in 2-3 seconds.**

No typing. No searching. No leaving the client.

---

## What It Knows

The ColorIq Voice Assistant isn't a generic chatbot. It's a **color science consultant** trained on:

- **3,273 professional shades** across 21 brands and 40+ color lines
- **Brand-specific processing times** — knows the difference between Shades EQ and Igora Royal
- **Underlying pigment data** — level 1 through 10
- **Client history** — texture, porosity, gray percentage, allergies, past formulas
- **Safety protocols** — PPD allergies, henna reactions, pregnancy considerations
- **Problem correction** — ashy toners, hot roots, banding, uneven porosity

## How It Works

1. **You speak** — push-to-talk, hands-free (designed for gloved hands)
2. **AI listens** — speech-to-text via browser (no install needed)
3. **AI consults** — checks your shade database, client history, formulation engine
4. **AI responds** — spoken answer + on-screen text
5. **Usage tracked** — transparent per-minute billing
