## Finding 5: AI + Non-Destructive Spectroscopy Replacing Traditional Antioxidant Tests (October 2026)

- **Source:** https://bioengineer.org/ai-and-non-destructive-spectroscopy-set-to-replace-century-old-antioxidant-tests/
- **Title:** "AI and Non-Destructive Spectroscopy Set to Replace Century-Old Antioxidant Tests"
- **Relevance:** MEDIUM
- **Summary:** Researchers are using machine learning classifiers across RGB, HSV, and CIELAB color spaces to analyze complex food emulsions via paper-based colorimetric tests. While the domain is food science, the methodology — ML models trained on CIELAB color-space data from camera/spectroscopy inputs — is directly transferable to hair color analysis. Paper mentions classifiers adapting to varying lighting conditions.
- **Product Relevance:** Supports ColorGenius's technical approach of using CIELAB + ML for photo-based hair color analysis. Validates that camera-based colorimetry under varying lighting is a solvable ML problem. Could inform improvements to the photo-analysis pipeline's robustness to salon lighting conditions.
- **Action Needed:** Review the paper for ML architecture details and lighting-normalization techniques that could improve ColorGenius's photo-analysis accuracy. Single-source claim; needs deeper technical review.
